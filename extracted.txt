IDEA TITLE
•
Problem Context: The solution addresses the "Adaptive Path Planning and 
Collision Avoidance for Autonomous Vehicles on Unstructured Indian Roads" 
challenge under the Smart Vehicles theme.
•
Core Concept: A dynamic, closed-loop simulation pipeline that abandons strict 
lane-based assumptions in favor of probabilistic, real-time trajectory mapping.
•
Uniqueness: The system specifically predicts the irregular, short-term motion of 
uniquely Indian road agents (e.g., auto-rickshaws, pushcarts, and pedestrians) and 
instantly recalculates safe paths when faced with sudden obstacles or missing lane 
markings.

TECHNICAL APPROACH
•
Scenario Generation: Use MathWorks RoadRunner to design the five critical test 
environments, including unmarked village roads, dense markets, and busy urban 
intersections without signals.
•
Perception Pipeline: Integrate the Automated Driving Toolbox for sensor fusion 
(combining simulated camera, LiDAR, and radar data) and the Deep Learning 
Toolbox to detect diverse traffic using datasets like the Indian Driving Dataset (IDD).
•
Decision & Planning: Implement the Navigation Toolbox to generate collision-free 
paths and Stateflow to design the state-machine logic required to handle abrupt 
events like sudden cattle crossings or informal highway merging.
•
Vehicle Dynamics: Use the Vehicle Dynamics Blockset (or a Simulink bicycle 
model) to accurately simulate vehicle physics and validate metrics like replanning 
latency and path smoothness.

RESEARCH  AND REFERENCES
"IDD: A Dataset for Exploring Problems of Autonomous Navigation in 
Unconstrained Environments" (Varma et al., WACV): This is the primary academic 
citation required if you utilize the IDD dataset for training your perception models.
"Explainable AI in Scene Understanding for Autonomous Vehicles in Unstructured 
Indian Roads" (MDPI): This paper proposes practical deep learning architectures for 
semantic segmentation on unstructured Indian roadways, specifically focusing on 
accurately segmenting drivable versus non-drivable areas in the absence of lane 
markings.
"Autonomous Ground Robot in Unstructured Environments: How Far Have We 
Come?" (Journal of Field Robotics, 2026): A comprehensive, up-to-date survey covering 
over 250 recent papers on environmental perception, real-time path planning, and 
non-lane-based end-to-end driving.
